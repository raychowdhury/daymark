Daymark support

Daymark is an iPhone habit tracker: check off daily habits or flexible weekly goals, run focus sessions, and look back at your week.

Get help

Open an issue at github.com/raychowdhury/daymark/issues. Describe what you did, what you expected, and your iPhone and iOS version. Please don't include personal information.

Common questions

  • Does finishing a focus timer complete my habit? No. Choose Log when a session ends. Minutes habits get the minutes you focused; other habits are marked complete.
  • What counts toward today's total? Habits scheduled for today. Flexible weekly goals have their own count and reset at the start of your week.
  • Why didn't I get a reminder? Turn on Reminder for the habit, allow notifications for Daymark in iPhone Settings, and check that a Focus mode isn't silencing it.
  • How do I move my data? Settings → Export backup creates a file you can import on another iPhone. iCloud sync is optional and off by default.
  • How do I delete my data? Settings → Erase all data, or delete the app.
