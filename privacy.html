Privacy

Last updated October 11, 2026

The short version

  • Daymark doesn't collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Your habits, check-ins, notes and focus sessions are stored on your iPhone.
  • If you turn on iCloud sync, a copy is kept in your own iCloud account, which we can't access.

What Daymark stores, and where

On your iPhone, in the app's private storage: your optional first name, your habits and schedules, daily check-ins and completion times, notes, focus sessions, and your settings.

Daymark does not send this data to us or to any third party. The app makes no network requests of its own.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

When you turn it on in Settings, Daymark saves a copy of your data with Apple's iCloud key-value storage under your Apple Account so your other devices can read it. Apple handles that storage under Apple's privacy policy. Turning sync off stops new copies being saved.

Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders and the session-end alert are scheduled on your iPhone. They include habit names, which may show on your Lock Screen depending on your notification settings. The Lock Screen focus timer is also created on your device.

Your choices

  • Export a backup or your history (CSV) from Settings at any time.
  • Erase everything from Settings → Erase all data, or by deleting the app.
  • Daymark is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact
